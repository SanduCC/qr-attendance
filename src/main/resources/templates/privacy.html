Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your first and last name and your email address.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID (a number that identifies your account, not your password).
  • If you sign in with a code sent to your email: your email address is what identifies your account. It is the same address as above; nothing else is stored.
  • The time you checked in to each class session.

When you sign in with email, we send you a one-time code through our email provider, which processes your address only to deliver that message, on our behalf (a data processor under the GDPR). The code is kept, as a one-way hash, only until it is used or for 10 minutes at most, and is never stored in our database.

Your IP address and a device identifier are stored only as salted one-way hashes. They can't be turned back into the original values. We use them only to stop check-in fraud, such as one phone checking in several people.

Some teachers turn on a location check. Then your browser asks to share your phone's location when you check in. It is compared once with the classroom's location and then discarded: we never store where you are.

If you can't scan, your teacher can add you to the list by hand. That entry holds only your name, the time and which teacher added it.

Who sees it

Only the teacher of the course sees its attendance list, and can correct a misspelled name. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until the end of the academic year. After that it is deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, or delete your data. Contact your teacher or your institution to do this.
