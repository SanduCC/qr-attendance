Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your first and last name and your email address.
  • Your Google account ID (a number that identifies your account, not your password).
  • The time you checked in to each class session.

Your IP address and a device identifier are stored only as salted one-way hashes. They can't be turned back into the original values. We use them only to stop check-in fraud, such as one phone checking in several people.

Who sees it

Only the teacher of the course sees its attendance list. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until the end of the academic year. After that it is deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, or delete your data. Contact your teacher or your institution to do this.
